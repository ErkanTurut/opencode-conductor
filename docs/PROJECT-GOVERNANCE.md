# Project governance contracts

These pure contracts do not configure Git, contact a provider, issue approvals, or authorize a shell command. Integrators must separately enforce existing package/user authority and pass its decision as `broaderAuthorityAllowed`. A project policy cannot grant authority. Missing or invalid policy denies remote operations. No force operation is allowed. Branches are literal names, never refspecs or revision expressions. Policies explicitly list repository, remotes, targets, operations, required checks, preview providers, and maximum observation age; serialize and hash the normalized policy with `serializeProjectPolicy` and `hashProjectPolicy`.

`decideProjectChange` requires authenticated, trusted-adapter `VerifiedApproval` and `VerifiedObservation` evidence, bound to operation, repository, remote, target, full candidate SHA, and policy hash. Approval includes human identity and expiry; a raw event's `approved=true` is not evidence. The adapter must independently authenticate identity, enforce human review (including phone review where applicable), and verify remote protections, check results and deployment data. Observations and preview must be fresh, and preview ready on the exact candidate. Recheck all authority and evidence immediately before execution; callers must address Git/ref races atomically in the execution layer. A boolean `verified` is a type-level integration contract, not cryptographic verification.

Preview URLs must be HTTPS without credentials, literal IPs, single-label or known local/internal names. Providers are explicitly policy-allowlisted, not guessed from hostnames. This validation does not resolve DNS, verify public routing, defend DNS rebinding, establish provider ownership, or authorize fetching a URL. Trusted provider adapters must verify deployment identity and host ownership and apply network/redirect restrictions; no Vercel or Cloudflare adapter is implemented here.

`reviewMemory` classifies legacy and current records using independently gathered content and source evidence. Missing provenance and invalid dates are not trusted. Changed sources or content are conflicts; expired review is stale. `reviewMemorySet` additionally suppresses direct superseded versions and flags contradictory eligible candidates. Supply the complete set, independently authenticated approval status and current evidence; the functions do not verify human approvals or hashes themselves. All ineligible results propose review, never overwrite memory. No config, GUI, state or network writes occur.
